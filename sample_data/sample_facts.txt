Tesla, Inc. is an American electric vehicle and clean energy company. It was incorporated on July 1, 2003, by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning. Elon Musk joined as chairman and later became CEO.

Tesla's headquarters is in Austin, Texas, having moved from Palo Alto, California in 2021.

The company produces electric vehicles including the Model S, Model 3, Model X, and Model Y. Tesla also manufactures battery energy storage systems, solar panels, and solar roof tiles.

Tesla went public on June 29, 2010, with an initial public offering (IPO) price of $17 per share on the NASDAQ stock exchange.

The Model 3 became the world's best-selling electric car in 2020. Tesla delivered approximately 1.31 million vehicles in 2022.

SpaceX is a separate company also led by Elon Musk, focused on space transportation. It was founded in 2002 and is headquartered in Hawthorne, California.

Albert Einstein developed the theory of general relativity in 1915. He was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921 for his discovery of the photoelectric effect.

The Python programming language was created by Guido van Rossum and first released in 1991. Python emphasizes code readability and uses significant whitespace.

The Great Wall of China is approximately 21,196 kilometers (13,171 miles) long. Construction began in the 7th century BC and continued for many centuries.
